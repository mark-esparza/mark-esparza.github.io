Digital Creator

Using YouTube and the internet to educate, inspire, and share what I know

"My heart is stirred by a noble theme as I recite my verses for the king; my tongue is the pen of a skillful writer." — Psalm 45:1

Why Content Creation?

The internet is the most powerful communication tool in history. I want to use it to share knowledge, document my journey, and connect with people who are interested in medicine, entrepreneurship, and building a purposeful life.

Content Topics

  • Medicine and medical school — what it's really like
  • Personal finance, real estate, and wealth-building
  • Personal development and productivity
  • Entrepreneurship and side income as a medical student
  • Travel vlogs from mission trips and global experiences
  • Faith, character, and living with purpose

Platforms & Goals

  • YouTube — long-form educational and lifestyle videos
  • Podcast — conversations on medicine, business, and life
  • Blog / newsletter — written content for deeper dives
  • Reach 10,000 subscribers within the first two years of consistent posting
  • Collaborate with other creators and professionals

Values as a Creator

Truth over clicks. Every video or post I put out will prioritize honesty and real value over engagement bait. I want an audience that trusts me — and that means never compromising the message for the algorithm.
