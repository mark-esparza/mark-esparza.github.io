Content Creator

Using YouTube and the internet to educate, inspire, and share faith with the world

"My heart is stirred by a noble theme as I recite my verses for the king; my tongue is the pen of a skillful writer." — Psalm 45:1

Why Content Creation?

The internet is the most powerful communication tool in history. I want to use it to share knowledge, encourage believers, challenge the culture with a thoughtful Christian voice, and document my journey — so others can learn from both my successes and my mistakes.

Content Topics

  • Faith and medicine — navigating healthcare with a Christian worldview
  • Bible study breakdowns and devotional content
  • Personal development from a biblical perspective
  • Entrepreneurship and stewardship for young Christians
  • Travel vlogs from mission trips and global adventures
  • Interviews with doctors, scholars, and Christian leaders

Platforms & Goals

  • YouTube — long-form educational and devotional videos
  • Podcast — conversations on faith, medicine, and life
  • Blog / newsletter — written content for deeper reflection
  • Reach 10,000 subscribers within the first two years of consistent posting
  • Collaborate with other Christian creators and medical professionals

Values as a Creator

Truth over clicks. Every video, post, or episode I produce will prioritize accuracy and integrity over engagement bait. I want an audience that trusts me — and that means never compromising the message for the algorithm.
