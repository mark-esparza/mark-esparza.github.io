Researcher

Using evidence and inquiry to push the boundaries of what we know

"It is the glory of God to conceal a matter; to search out a matter is the glory of kings." — Proverbs 25:2

Why Research?

Medicine treats today's patients. Research helps tomorrow's. God calls us to be good stewards of the world He created — and that includes advancing our understanding of disease, healing, and human health. I want to contribute to knowledge that serves communities often overlooked in clinical studies.

Research Interests

  • Health disparities in minority and underserved communities
  • Preventative medicine and lifestyle interventions
  • Christian bioethics in clinical practice
  • Global infectious disease and epidemiology
  • Mental health outcomes and the role of faith and community

Goals

  • Participate in undergraduate and medical school research programs
  • Publish peer-reviewed papers in relevant fields
  • Pursue an MD/PhD or research fellowship if the path aligns
  • Present findings at national and international conferences
  • Explore the intersection of faith, ethics, and clinical research
