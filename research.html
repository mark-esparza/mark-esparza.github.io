Researcher

Using evidence and inquiry to push the boundaries of what we know

Why Research?

Medicine treats today's patients. Research helps tomorrow's. I want to contribute to the body of knowledge that shapes how diseases are understood, prevented, and cured — with a focus on communities that are underrepresented in clinical studies.

Research Interests

  • Health disparities in minority and underserved communities
  • Preventative medicine and lifestyle interventions
  • Global infectious disease and epidemiology
  • Mental health outcomes and the role of community and support systems
  • Medical ethics in clinical practice

Goals

  • Participate in research programs during medical school
  • Publish peer-reviewed papers in relevant fields
  • Pursue an MD/PhD or research fellowship if the path aligns
  • Present findings at national and international conferences
  • Use research to directly improve care for underserved populations
