Student of Knowledge

Pursuing biblical wisdom and lifelong learning as a foundation for everything I do

"The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding." — Proverbs 9:10

What This Means to Me

Being a student of knowledge means committing to a lifelong journey of learning — rooted in Scripture and guided by faith. It is the foundation upon which all my other goals rest. I want to grow in wisdom so I can lead, serve, and teach others effectively.

Areas of Study

  • Bible study — Old and New Testament theology
  • Christian apologetics and worldview
  • Church history and the writings of the Church Fathers
  • Biblical ethics — applied to medicine, business, and everyday life
  • Systematic theology and Christian philosophy
  • Discipleship and spiritual formation

Short-Term Goals

  • Complete a structured Bible reading plan cover to cover
  • Join or lead a small group Bible study
  • Read foundational Christian works (C.S. Lewis, Spurgeon, Tozer)
  • Establish a daily devotional and prayer routine

Long-Term Vision

To be a person of deep faith and biblical literacy who bridges Christian scholarship with modern professional life — making the wisdom of Scripture accessible and practical for believers navigating medicine, business, and everyday challenges.
