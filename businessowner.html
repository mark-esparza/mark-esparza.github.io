Business Owner

Building enterprises in the US and globally that create value and reflect Christian integrity

"Commit to the Lord whatever you do, and he will establish your plans." — Proverbs 16:3

Why Entrepreneurship?

Business ownership creates freedom — the freedom to employ others, give generously, pursue meaningful work, and build something that lasts. I want to run businesses that are profitable and principled, where every decision reflects the integrity God calls us to in every area of life.

Business Ideas & Interests

  • Healthcare-adjacent services (telehealth, wellness, medical education)
  • Faith-based publishing and media
  • Community development ventures in underserved areas
  • Import/export or trade businesses with a global reach
  • Technology tools for education and productivity

Core Principles

  • Honesty and transparency in all dealings
  • Fair treatment and generous compensation for employees
  • A portion of profits dedicated to tithing and charitable giving
  • Excellence as a form of worship — doing everything to a high standard

Goals

  • Launch a first small business while still in school or residency
  • Build at least one US-based business to sustainable profitability
  • Expand internationally — particularly in Africa or underserved global markets
  • Create jobs and mentor other young Christian entrepreneurs
