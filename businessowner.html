Why Build a Business?

Business ownership creates freedom — the freedom to employ others, give generously, pursue meaningful work, and build something that lasts. I want to run businesses that are profitable and principled, where integrity isn't optional.

Business Ideas & Interests

  • Healthcare-adjacent services (telehealth, wellness, medical education)
  • Publishing and media
  • Community development ventures in underserved areas
  • Import/export or trade businesses with a global reach
  • Technology tools for education and productivity

Core Principles

  • Honesty and transparency in all dealings
  • Fair treatment and good compensation for employees
  • A portion of profits dedicated to giving back
  • Excellence in everything — doing the work right the first time

Goals

  • Launch a first business while still in school or residency
  • Build at least one US-based business to sustainable profitability
  • Expand internationally — particularly in Africa or underserved global markets
  • Create jobs and mentor other young entrepreneurs
