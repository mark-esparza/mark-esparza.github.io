Real Estate

Building wealth through property to create lasting security and enable generosity

Why Real Estate?

Real estate is one of the most reliable wealth-building tools available. The Bible calls us to be faithful stewards of what God entrusts to us. Building a strong property portfolio creates the ability to provide housing, support community initiatives, and fund charitable and missionary work for generations.

Strategy

  • Approach investments with biblical principles of stewardship and integrity
  • Start with residential rental properties to build steady cash flow
  • Reinvest profits into additional properties over time
  • Explore commercial real estate and mixed-use developments
  • Consider international property markets, including in regions where mission work is active

Milestones

  • Learn the fundamentals: financing, contracts, valuations, and market analysis
  • Purchase a first rental property after graduating
  • Grow to a portfolio of 5+ properties within 10 years of graduating
  • Use rental income to fund charitable, church, and philanthropic projects
