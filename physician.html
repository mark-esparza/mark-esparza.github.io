Practicing Physician

Combining medical expertise with compassionate, faith-guided patient care

"He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds." — Psalm 147:3

Why Medicine?

Medicine is one of the most direct ways to serve humanity. Jesus himself healed the sick and called us to care for others. Every patient is made in the image of God, and the ability to relieve suffering and restore health is a profound privilege and calling.

Areas of Interest

  • Internal medicine and primary care
  • Global health and underserved communities
  • Integrative and preventative medicine
  • Christian bioethics — end-of-life care, human dignity, and reproductive health
  • Mental health and its intersection with faith and spiritual wellbeing

The Road Ahead

  • Complete pre-medical requirements with strong academic performance
  • Gain clinical volunteering and shadowing experience
  • Prepare for and excel on the MCAT
  • Attend and graduate from medical school
  • Complete residency in a chosen specialty
  • Serve in mission trips and underserved clinics globally

The Physician I Want to Be

A doctor who listens before prescribing, who treats the whole person — body and soul — and who makes time for patients regardless of their background or ability to pay. My faith and my medicine are not separate; they reinforce each other in every patient encounter.
