Oklahoma State University — 2nd Year Medical Student

Where I Am Now

I am currently a 2nd year medical student at Oklahoma State University College of Osteopathic Medicine. I'm deep in foundational coursework and preparing for board exams, staying focused on becoming the best physician I can be.

Why Medicine?

Medicine is one of the most direct ways to serve people. The ability to relieve suffering, restore health, and walk alongside patients through some of the hardest moments of their lives is a privilege I don't take lightly. I was drawn to this field by a genuine desire to help — and that hasn't changed.

Areas of Interest

  • Internal medicine and primary care
  • Global health and underserved communities
  • Integrative and preventative medicine
  • Mental health and whole-person care
  • Medical ethics and patient advocacy

The Road Ahead

  • Pass COMLEX Level 1 and Level 2 board exams
  • Complete clinical rotations across core specialties
  • Match into a residency program aligned with my interests
  • Complete residency and begin practicing
  • Serve in mission trips and underserved clinics globally

The Physician I Want to Be

A doctor who listens before prescribing, treats the whole person, and makes time for every patient regardless of their background. I believe good medicine is both a science and a calling — and I intend to honor both.
