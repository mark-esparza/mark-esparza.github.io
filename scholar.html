Author

Writing books and guides that illuminate, educate, and inspire through a biblical lens

"Of making many books there is no end, and much study wearies the body." — Ecclesiastes 12:12

Why Write?

A well-written book can reach people across centuries and continents. I want to put knowledge into forms that outlast me — practical guides, thoughtful essays, and accessible writing that helps Christians navigate modern life with wisdom, courage, and biblical grounding.

Topics I Want to Write About

  • Christian guidance for healthcare professionals
  • Biblical principles in modern business and finance
  • Faith and personal development for young Christian men
  • Health and wellness through a biblical lens
  • Apologetics — defending and explaining the Christian faith

Writing Goals

  • Start a structured writing habit — even 15 minutes a day
  • Publish long-form articles and essays online
  • Complete a first draft manuscript within 5 years
  • Collaborate with Christian publishers and ministries
  • Build a readership through consistent, quality, faith-grounded output
